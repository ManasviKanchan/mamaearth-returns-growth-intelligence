Situation

Regional operations and finance teams analyzed recent financial and logistical performance metrics. True peak month performance occurred in March 2026, generating a revenue of 20318.9 INR. Across the broader operating window, raw total revenue reached 99860.2 INR, which included a duplicate reconciliation delta of 2501.9 INR, yielding a cleaned total revenue of 97358.3 INR. 

Complication

Significant fulfillment risks emerged around payment methods and order channels. Return rates varied widely by payment type, with CARD at 14.7 percent, UPI at 18.9 percent, and COD at 44.4 percent. The highest risk segment was isolated to COD orders in city tier 2, registering an alarming return rate of 54.5 percent. Additionally, data integrity issues distorted monthly trends; January 2026 appeared as an apparent revenue peak of 29582.1 INR due to anomalies, but outlier-corrected monthly revenue for January 2026 stood at 11637.1 INR. Analysts note that observed associations do not prove causation.

Resolution

To improve regional logistics and financial forecasting, operations teams should prioritize investigating the high return rates associated with cash-on-delivery orders, particularly in Tier-2 cities. Before implementing policy changes, teams should examine potential operational causes and evaluate targeted interventions. Finance teams should consistently reconcile duplicate transactions and use cleaned revenue of 97358.3 INR for reporting. Monthly performance should be evaluated using outlier-corrected figures, including January 2026 revenue of 11637.1 INR, to avoid misleading conclusions from unusual order quantities.
